\cventry{Fixed-Wing UAV Hardware Development}{2025 - Present}
\cvsubline{Independent Project}
\begin{cvitems}
  \item Develop a fixed-wing UAV through iterative CAD, additive-manufacturing, and hardware-integration cycles, applying experience from assembling, repairing, and flight-testing RC aircraft.
  \item Modified wing geometry with winglets and reinforced internal structures to improve strength and support the intended aircraft configuration.
  \item Designed the fuselage and internal layout around the flight controller, battery, ESC, wiring, and payload while managing centre-of-gravity, accessibility, and electromagnetic-interference considerations.
  \item Introduced removable access features for electronics and servos and simplified component interfaces to improve assembly, maintenance, and design iteration.
  \item Integrated ArduPilot-compatible hardware, including IMU, GPS, airspeed sensing, telemetry, servos, ESC, and power distribution.
  \item Used flight testing of RC platforms to investigate oscillatory and overly sensitive behaviour and inform subsequent control-system and configuration iterations.
  \item Applied topology optimisation and 3D-printing parameter studies to develop landing-gear and structural concepts while addressing support strategy, print quality, and material shrinkage.
\end{cvitems}
