\cventry{Development of Fixed-Wing UAV}{From Feb. 2025}
\begin{cvitems}
  \item Performed iterative structural modifications following flight-test observations and landing loads.
  \item Balanced structural stiffness, mass and manufacturability throughout UAV development.
  \item Integrated structural and flight-system design to improve overall aircraft robustness.
  \item Defined system architecture including IMU, GPS, airspeed sensor, flight controller, and telemetry systems.
  \item Developed and tuned control loops (PID) to achieve stable and predictable flight behavior under real-world disturbances.
  \item Conducted iterative flight testing following a structured development cycle (design $\rightarrow$ build $\rightarrow$ fly $\rightarrow$ refine).
  \item Integrated onboard electronics including ESCs, servos, RC systems, and power distribution for reliable actuation and communication.
  \item Analyzed flight data and system response to improve robustness against sensor noise, latency, and environmental effects such as wind.
\end{cvitems}
